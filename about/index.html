Our Story

About Local SME

Connecting communities with the best of local businesses — one catalogue at a time.

We believe that strong local economies build stronger communities. Our platform gives small businesses a digital storefront and gives customers a way to discover, support, and celebrate the makers next door.

Our Mission

To empower local small and medium enterprises with an affordable, modern digital presence — helping them reach new customers, showcase their products, and grow sustainably within their communities.

Our Vision

A thriving network of local businesses where every neighbourhood has access to a vibrant online marketplace — and where shopping local becomes the first choice, not the last resort.

What We Stand For

Community First
